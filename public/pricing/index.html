What affects the price?

Every home is different, so we price by the work involved, not a one-size-fits-all rate. The biggest factors are:

  • Square footage of the home
  • Number of bedrooms and bathrooms
  • The type of cleaning (standard, deep, or move-out)
  • The current condition of the home
  • How often you schedule service

Pricing questions

How much does house cleaning cost in St. George?

Our standard cleaning starts at $120 and deep cleaning starts at $200. The final price depends on square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, and the condition of the home. Try our cleaning cost estimator for an instant range.

How are move-out and commercial cleanings priced?

Both are quoted individually because every property is different. Move-out quotes depend on the size and condition of the empty home; commercial quotes depend on your space and schedule.
