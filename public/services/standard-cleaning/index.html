FAQ

Standard Cleaning FAQ

How often should I schedule a standard cleaning?

Most households choose every week or every other week. Monthly works well for smaller homes or people who tidy in between. Tell us what you have in mind and we will build a schedule around it.

What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning covers the surfaces you see and use every day. Deep cleaning adds the detail work: inside cabinets, baseboards hand-wiped, window sills, blinds, vents, and grout. If your home has not been professionally cleaned in a while, we usually suggest starting with a deep clean.
