Booking & estimates

How do I book a cleaning?

Call or text Rose or Maria at (435) 301-4337, email us, or send a request through our contact page. We respond the same day, and we will set up a time that works for you.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Tell us about your home or business and we will give you an honest number upfront, with no pressure and no surprise charges later.

What are your hours?

We clean Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Commercial schedules are set with each client.

Pricing

How much does house cleaning cost in St. George?

Our standard cleaning starts at $120 and deep cleaning starts at $200. The final price depends on square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, and the condition of the home. Try our cleaning cost estimator for an instant range.

How are move-out and commercial cleanings priced?

Both are quoted individually because every property is different. Move-out quotes depend on the size and condition of the empty home; commercial quotes depend on your space and schedule.

Our services

What is the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A standard clean covers the everyday surfaces: dusting, floors, kitchen, and bathrooms. A deep clean adds detail work like inside cabinets, baseboards, blinds, vents, window sills, and grout.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. Most of our residential clients book standard cleaning on a regular schedule, such as weekly, every other week, or monthly. Commercial clients can set a custom schedule.

Do you clean offices and businesses?

Yes. We provide janitorial service for offices and small businesses, including restrooms, workspaces, floors, and trash removal, on a schedule that fits your hours.

Trust & safety

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Rose’s Cleaning & Janitorial is a licensed and insured business, so you and your home are protected.

Who will be cleaning my home?

Rose and Maria, the owners. You will not get a rotating crew of strangers. We are a local family business, and we treat every home like our own.

Service areas

What areas do you serve?

We are based in St. George and serve Washington County, including Washington, Santa Clara, Ivins, and Hurricane. Not sure if you are in range? Just ask.
